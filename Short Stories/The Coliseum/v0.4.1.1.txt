Every sound feels amplified—the crunch of gravel under my boots as the dust clings, soft and relentless, as if trying to drag me back into the earth. , the whisper of the wind through cracks in the walls. Or maybe it’s just the routine itself that keeps me coming back, the act of searching when I don’t know how to stop.
