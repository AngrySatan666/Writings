The night air is heavy with the smell of damp stone and earth, the wind pulling at my cloak, cold and firm, pressing against my skin like the weight of unanswered questions. I walk the same path I’ve walked countless nights. Every sound feels amplified—the crunch of gravel under my boots, the whisper of wind threading through cracks in the walls. Dust clings to my feet, soft and relentless, as if trying to drag me back into the earth. I stalk the perimeter of the old warrior’s coliseum like a hunter circling its prey, though what I’m hunting, I still don’t know.

My hands are shoved deep in my pockets, fingers brushing the crumbs of dirt I picked up the last time I came here. The ruins rise ahead of me, silhouetted against the hollow glow of the moon, their jagged edges blurred by the haze of midnight fog. A flicker of moonlight glances off a broken pillar, and for a moment, I pause, my breath catching in my throat. Is it treasure I’m searching for? Or just proof that something worth finding ever existed here? Maybe it’s something I’ve convinced myself is hidden here—something precious, something that will finally make all of this make sense.

Or maybe it’s just the routine that keeps me coming back. The act of searching, even when I no longer know what I’m looking for. I keep telling myself it’s a hidden spark of meaning I seek, but the truth is, I’ve walked this path so many times now that I’ve forgotten what I’m even searching for. I only know I can’t stop.
