# Project 666: Chems & Mechanisms

## Chemical References

### Citric Acid (C6H8O7) [CA]
- **BallNStick:** C6H8O7
- **Structure:** HOOC–CH2–C(OH)(COOH)–CH2–COOH
- **Molecular Weight:** 192.12 g/mol
- **Density:** ~1.66 g/cm³ (solid, anhydrous at 20 °C)
- **Melting Point:** 153 °C (decomposes, no true boiling point)

---

### Calcium Hypochlorite (Ca(OCl)2) [CaHy]
- **BallNStick:** Ca(OCl)2
- **Structure:** Ca2+ with 2× OCl-
- **Molecular Weight:** 142.98 g/mol (anhydrous)
- **Density:** ~2.35 g/cm³ (solid)
- **Decomposition:** ~175 °C (no normal boiling point)

---

### Chlorine Gas (Cl2) [Cl2]
- **BallNStick:** Cl2
- **Structure:** Cl–Cl (diatomic)
- **Molecular Weight:** 70.90 g/mol
- **Density:** ~3.21 g/L (STP) = 0.00321 g/cm³
- **Boiling Point:** −34.04 °C

---

# Stoichiometric Quick-Reference (theoretical)

## Using Hydrochloric Acid (HCl, strong monoprotic) 4:1

  - ![HCl Equation](https://latex.codecogs.com/svg.latex?\Large\;Ca(OCl)_{2}+4HCl\;\to\;CaCl_{2}+2Cl_{2}(g)+2H_{2}O)

### 1 Calcium Hypochlorite : 4 Hydrochloric Acid
### Per 1 mol Ca(OCl)₂
| Acid (proton source) | H⁺ per mol acid | Acid needed (mol) | Cl₂ formed (mol) | H₂O formed (mol) | Typical Ca product* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| HCl | 1 | **4.000** | **2.000** | **2.000** | CaCl₂(aq) |
| Citric acid (H₃A) | 3 | **1.333** (=4/3) | **2.000** | **2.000** | Ca²⁺ + citrate species / Ca-citrate(s)** |

\* Actual calcium speciation depends on pH/ionic strength; CaCl₂ dominates with HCl; citrate can complex/precipitate with H₃A.  
\** Calcium citrate formula often written Ca₃(C₆H₅O₇)₂ (limited solubility).

## Using Citric Acid (H₃A, triprotic) 4:3

  - ![Citric Acid Equation](https://latex.codecogs.com/svg.latex?\Large\;3Ca(OCl)_{2}+4C_{6}H_{8}O_{7}\;\to\;6Cl_{2}(g)+6H_{2}O+Ca^{2+}/\text{citrate})

### 3 Calcium Hypochlorite : 4 Citric Acid Solution
### Per 1 mol Acid
| Acid | Ca(OCl)₂ consumed (mol) | Cl₂ formed (mol) | H₂O formed (mol) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| HCl | **0.250** (=1/4) | **0.500** | **0.500** |
| Citric acid (H₃A) | **0.750** (=3/4) | **1.500** | **1.500** |

Notes:
- Core rule: **4 H⁺ → 2 Cl₂** per **1 Ca(OCl)₂**.
- Waters of hydration in commercial Ca(OCl)₂·xH₂O do **not** change the H⁺/Cl₂ stoichiometry—only masses.
